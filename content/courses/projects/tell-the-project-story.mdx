## Explain the decisions behind the demo

A case study connects your problem, implementation and evaluation. Keep it focused: who the work was for, what you built, your contribution, an important tradeoff, how you checked it and what remains incomplete. Include a demo or screenshots and a link to reproducible setup when possible.

Describe outcomes at the scale you actually observed. “Three classmates tested the prototype” is different from “used by the campus.” Local benchmarks need their conditions. Team projects need clear ownership and credit. Be ready to explain one failure and how you investigated it.

Practice a short walkthrough rather than memorizing every line. Start with the user's task, show the normal path and explain a decision. Then invite questions. If the demo relies on a service, prepare a recorded fallback and disclose when it was recorded.
